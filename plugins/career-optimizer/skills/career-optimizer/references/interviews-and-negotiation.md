# Interviews, debriefs and negotiation

Read the Profile, relevant Experience, target Opportunity and linked Activities.
Use `notion-schema.md` for record structure and `writing-and-qa.md` for drafts.

## Interview preparation

Confirm stage, format, date, language and known interviewers from user-provided
or authoritative information. Research relevant current company information
from public sources. Treat likely questions as hypotheses; do not assert an
interviewer's intentions or infer private characteristics from public profiles.

Build a focused pack: motivation and CV walkthrough; behavioral competencies;
role-specific technical questions; honest gap answers; and questions for the
employer about success, mandate, collaboration and conditions that matter.
Adapt to the actual stage and user's experience, including early career,
career changes, specialist, management or executive roles.

For behavioral stories, use situation, task, personal action and result.
Link to the underlying Experience evidence and disclosure permissions.
Do not manufacture a failure, conflict or leadership story to fill a matrix.
If a story lacks a result, ask or keep the result unknown. Prepare a main and
backup story where evidence permits, with different emphasis for the audience.
For gaps, acknowledge the limit, show relevant adjacent evidence and explain a
realistic learning plan. Do not imply transferable evidence is direct experience.

Run mocks one question at a time. Give brief feedback on clarity, evidence,
relevance and length; suggest an improved answer grounded in what the user said.
Use follow-ups to test ownership and weak assumptions. Finish with a few useful
practice priorities. Rehearse in the requested language without overstating fluency.

## Debrief and learning

Capture interviewer/stage, questions, user observations, stated next steps and
timing while fresh. Separate observed statements from the user's interpretation
and the assistant's inference; positive tone does not prove an offer is coming.
When logging is requested, save a linked Activity and update only confirmed
Opportunity changes. Record recurring questions or story improvements in the
existing records; do not create a local file as a second source of truth.
Draft a thank-you or follow-up when useful, without sending it automatically.

## Offer and compensation capture

Use the written offer or the user's reported terms. Identify currency, location,
employment type, full-time basis and payment schedule. Ask only for material
missing terms and respect the Profile's compensation-storage preference.
Capture base, bonus conditions, signing payments, pension/retirement contributions,
equity/options/vesting, leave, hours, flexibility, title, start date, notice,
probation and material restrictions as relevant. Do not invent standard terms.

Normalize comparable amounts with explicit assumptions:

- Guaranteed recurring cash: annual base plus truly guaranteed recurring amounts.
  A thirteenth payment may already be included in stated annual base; check.
- Variable cash: target and downside/upside scenarios, with payout conditions.
  A target bonus is not guaranteed income.
- First-year items: signing/relocation payments and repayment conditions shown
  separately from recurring cash.
- Equity/options: instrument, vesting, exercise cost, valuation/liquidity and
  forfeiture assumptions. Paper value is not spendable or guaranteed cash.
- Benefits/pension: separate employer contributions and noncash benefits from
  cash pay; avoid double-counting or assuming immediate accessibility.

Do not collapse these into one certain "total compensation" figure. Show the
components and scenarios. For currency conversion use a dated rate; distinguish
gross compensation from estimated net pay. Verify current tax, employment-law,
permit or pension claims with authoritative jurisdiction-specific sources.

## Benchmarks and negotiation

Research current role/level/location benchmarks when requested or needed.
Use more than one credible source when available; state dates, sample limits,
base versus package definitions and uncertain comparability. If reliable data
is unavailable, say so instead of inventing a market range.

Clarify priorities, minimum acceptable terms, target and real alternatives.
Salary-history disclosure rules and customs vary; verify relevant rules rather
than recommending disclosure or a history document by default.
Prepare a prioritized counter grounded in actual contribution and package needs.
Use competing offers or processes only if real and accurately described.
Provide a concise call script or email draft in the user's voice, with no bluffing.
Suggest written terms and reasonable decision time based on the actual deadline;
do not impose a universal waiting period or acceptance rule.

For multiple offers, compare guaranteed cash, variable upside, equity risk,
mandate, growth, work conditions and user priorities. A weighted scorecard is
optional; label provisional weights and test sensitivity. The user decides.
When authorized, log confirmed terms, negotiation state and resolution in the
existing Notion records. Never accept, decline, resign, send a counter or contact
an employer automatically. Sensitive pay details are not stored by default.
